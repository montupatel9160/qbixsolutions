Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • User Control & Transparency

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide aggregated analytics to site administrators.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

Necessary for basic website security, responsive navigation, form submission tokens, and cookie choice preservation.

Analytical & Performance Cookies

Allows us to count visits and traffic sources (e.g. Google Analytics 4) so we can measure and optimize the speed and usability of our website. All data is aggregated and anonymized.

Marketing & Social Cookies

May be set through our site by marketing partners (such as Meta Pixel and LinkedIn Insight Tag) to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant agency offerings on other platforms.

3. Controlling Your Cookies

You can modify your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the button below or configuring your browser to block or alert you about cookies.
