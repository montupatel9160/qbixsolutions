1. Scope of Engagement

Qbix Solutions provides professional digital marketing, web engineering, graphic design, and search engine optimization services as specified in each custom Scope of Work (SOW) or proposal issued to the client.

2. Milestone Payments & Invoicing

Unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing, project work follows our balanced milestone structure:

  • 30% Advance: Upon contract execution and discovery phase kickoff.
  • 40% Midpoint: Upon client approval of UI/UX design wireframes or functional demo build.
  • 30% Launch: Upon final quality testing, client signoff, and live server deployment.

Retainers (such as monthly SEO and Social Media Management) are invoiced monthly at the start of each billing cycle.

Upon full settlement of all project invoices, the client receives 100% full commercial ownership of all custom website designs, logos, content, and application code specifically created for their project. Qbix Solutions provides 3 months of complimentary warranty support following project delivery. This covers fixing defects, code bugs, and minor text/image corrections. Ongoing maintenance packages are available thereafter.
