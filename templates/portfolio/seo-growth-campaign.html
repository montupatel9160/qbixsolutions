Project Overview & Scope

Our client was suffering from severe index bloat, duplicate canonical redirect chains, and missing structured data, preventing high-intent commercial landing pages from achieving top-tier ranking positions.

Key Deliverables & Execution Highlights

  • Technical Crawl Architecture: Audit of 10,000+ URLs using Screaming Frog, fixing 404 broken links, eliminating redirect chains, and optimizing robots.txt.
  • Schema.org Structured Data: Sitewide implementation of Organization, WebPage, Article, Product, and LocalBusiness JSON-LD markup for enhanced Google SERP rich snippets.
  • Entity Keyword Mapping: Reclustering 80+ high-commercial intent keywords into topical pillars with clear search intent hierarchy.
  • Search Performance Monitoring: Bi-weekly tracking of impression surges, average CTR improvements, and keyword position mobility via Google Search Console.

Commercial Results & Technical Impact

Scaled organic impressions to over 2.1 million monthly views, propelling 40+ high-value commercial keywords into Google India's top 3 positions with an overall +240% organic traffic surge.
