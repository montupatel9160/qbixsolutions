Project Overview & Scope

Physio Life Clinic, operated by Dr. Nildeep Dava, required a reliable and intuitive digital touchpoint where patients could understand specialized therapy treatments, verify practitioner qualifications, and schedule appointments without friction.

Key Deliverables & Execution Highlights

  • Patient Timeslot Booking: Clean appointment scheduling workflow with form validation, confirmation notifications, and doctor schedule coordination.
  • Service & Treatment Showcases: Categorized treatment guides detailing physiotherapy rehabilitation techniques, pain relief modalities, and recovery timelines.
  • Local Search Visibility: Optimized local schema markup and Google Business Profile integration for clinic searches in Morbi and Gujarat.
  • Mobile-First Responsive Layout: 100% accessible on iOS and Android devices, ensuring elderly patients and family members can navigate with ease.

Commercial Results & Technical Impact

The portal successfully digitized appointment requests, reducing phone-based scheduling overhead while boosting clinic discovery in regional search results.
